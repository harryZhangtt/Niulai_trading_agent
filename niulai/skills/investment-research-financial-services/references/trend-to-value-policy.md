# Trend-to-value execution contract

Version: 2026-10-21-trend-value-1

For every full company research or material valuation revision, identify the three most decision-relevant industry/company trends. Select by potential owner-profit impact, evidence quality and uncertainty, not by positivity. Allow fewer with an explicit reason; retain additional material risks. Do not force three bullish narratives or discard company-specific idiosyncrasies. For narrow followups apply only relevant parts and do not claim a new full valuation.

For each trend record in a `trend_value_bridge` artifact: dated primary evidence and counterevidence; measurement units and denominator; industry adoption versus company share; technical/customer advantage and economical replication risk; timing and falsifier; explicit model parameter paths; contribution to revenue, operating earnings, cash investment, financing and per-share owner value; priced-in expectations and how inferred; incremental unpriced contribution or an identified gap. Link this artifact from valuation_evidence.business_values and probability_basis. Unknown profitability or market expectations remain unknown, not zero.

Build a causal driver chain: activity × resource intensity ÷ productivity × addressable adoption × achievable share × realized price; map to margins, working capital, capital expenditure, funding and dilution. Distinguish sockets, cores, vCPUs, installed base and new shipments. Supplier benchmarks and TAM estimates are claims, not neutral forecasts. Address capacity ceilings, utilization/yield thresholds, learning curves, substitution, crowding-out and ramp delays. Use nonlinear structure only with economic justification; more complexity and decimal places do not establish accuracy.

Model joint states with shared drivers and constraints. Never add independent bullish uplifts or multiply dependent success probabilities. For each material trend run baseline, change-only, leave-one-out and feasible combined cases. Report combined-minus-individual interaction and avoid double counting internal revenues, shared plants, packaging and licensing. If allocating interaction using Shapley values, identify it as mathematical attribution conditional on a chosen baseline, not causal proof. Impossible combinations must be excluded or explicitly labeled stress tests.

Compare our dated unit/margin/timing assumptions with accessible consensus or price-implied conditions; never call reverse valuation observed consensus. Rank variant perceptions by evidence, financial materiality and catalyst. Include strongest market-friendly case and adverse joint case. Use reference-class probabilities only when sample/regime support exists; otherwise label judgment weights and show ranges and buy/wait flip thresholds. Backtest forecast errors when comparable records exist; prefer out-of-sample checks, avoid fitting probabilities to current price or desired action.

Independent reviewer must inspect trend sources, driver/model links, interaction and priced-in claims before full publication. A missing bridge is an incomplete full research gate; conditional wait with explicit evidence gaps is allowed under existing policy. This instruction is a workflow requirement, not a platform execution guarantee. Preserve prior records and never retroactively certify old research.
