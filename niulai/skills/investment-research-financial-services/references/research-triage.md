# Cost-aware research entry policy

Version 2026-10-20-triage-1. User-authorized cost target: approximately80% less token expenditure than full research on every candidate. This is a target, not measured savings or a rejection quota.

## Apply before any scheduled research claim or expensive source call
Read current configuration and user steering first. A held run remains unfinished; never auto-resume it merely because its lease expired. Preserve frozen identities and partial evidence. A new screening disposition is not full research completion or a supersession of an uncovered old run.

For batch candidates and scheduled checks, run scripts/research_triage.py using evidenced JSON inputs. The script only routes supplied facts; it does not verify sources, fetch news, certify baseline freshness, dispatch agents, alter holdings/alerts or provide recommendations. Every true factual flag requires source/check provenance. Unknown is not false. Record routing reason, data cutoff, baseline/price/source/model revisions and reopening condition. Preserve actual token usage if exposed; otherwise record unknown, not a proxy advertised as actual savings.

1. Repair incorrect security identity, split/share-class mapping or stale quotes before valuing. Verified outside-universe names may be excluded from the current screen; holdings risk monitoring remains separate.
2. Reuse a completed applicable baseline only after a bounded primary-source delta scan covers the interval since its evidence cutoff and confirms no material event. Repeated below-threshold observations in the same zone do not trigger full research. Cache unchanged documents and key shared industry evidence.
3. Reprice verified existing models deterministically when only price/time changes. A leveraged fund requires current path/volatility/cost mechanics, not a fixed stock-price discount. If the action boundary changes, review the changed decision and affected assumptions rather than rewrite the whole report.
4. Perform a bounded probe when no baseline exists or coverage is uncertain: latest results/filing, security/capital structure, economic unit, rough scenario envelope and decisive missing fact. Initially cap at two primary documents and a compact evidence card; missing evidence yields deferred_data, not a negative company verdict. Escalate scope only with a concrete decision-relevance reason.
5. Prefer a targeted delta review for material new orders, guidance, dilution, financing, competitive changes or unit economics. Rebuild fully for security/model-regime changes, consequential contradictions, or a promising decision whose baseline cannot support a recommendation. Existing-position solvency/thesis-break events take priority over cheap entry screens.

## Strong gates, not universal ratio vetoes

## Mandatory filter order for every candidate batch

Apply the same frozen ordering to future batches; the current eleven names are regression examples, not a training target. A filter suppresses unnecessary work, not necessarily the security. Preserve a distinct disposition and reopening condition.

| Order | Filter and minimum evidence | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Existing holding with a material solvency, covenant, dilution, fraud or thesis-break event | Priority risk review even when identity/quote needs simultaneous repair; never suppress with valuation or budget filters. |
| 1 | Verified instrument, share class, split basis, universe and quote timestamp | Repair/quarantine unknown identity or stale data. Exclude from this universe only with affirmative scope evidence. |
| 2 | Same event/zone, applicable reviewed baseline, and covered primary-source delta interval | Reuse unchanged work; reprice if price/time alone changed. An empty search or a repeated below-line price is not evidence of unchanged fundamentals. |
| 3 | Explicit material driver change: paid adoption, volume/pricing, incremental margin, retained capital, financing or claims | Update only affected model dependencies. Model-regime failure requires deeper rebuild. |
| 4 | Fresh reviewed broad optimistic per-share wealth envelope, reconciled claims/dilution, horizon and risk-appropriate hurdle | Defer for price only if that envelope also misses the hurdle. Include credible option/narrative value; store calculation and independent review. Unknown envelope means bounded probe, not rejection. |
| 5 | Remaining question could alter buy/wait/size or reveal holding risk | Fund a bounded targeted task with the decisive question and stopping condition. Otherwise wait for the named event/data/price condition, retaining monitoring. |

Prioritize four economic bridges rather than universal ratio thresholds: (a) demand -> paid customer spending -> revenue, (b) incremental revenue -> incremental owner profit after inference/production/support costs, (c) profit -> cash after necessary reinvestment and working capital, and (d) enterprise value -> common per-share value after debt, minorities, dilution and preferred claims. Financial firms require credit losses, funding and regulatory capital; cyclicals require mid-cycle utilization/pricing; funds require underlying exposure, daily reset, financing and path distributions. Each bridge may route a specific question without demanding a full model in the cheap stage.

Do not label negative FCF as structural cash destruction without separating growth investment, financing, cycle and maintenance needs. Do not reject a high multiple without a credible broad forward envelope. Do not reject a missing disclosure as an adverse fact. Support/resistance is an execution overlay, not a business-quality gate.

The script enforces evidence references before accepting unchanged-baseline reuse or price deferral. Its checks establish input completeness only; the independent reviewer verifies source content and model reasonableness. Keep risk-monitoring coverage even for deferred names. Record actual token costs and audit false negatives on future, time-separated samples; do not tune to an 80% rejection rate.
The strongest low-cost gates are duplicate event/baseline validity, correct security identity, and distance from a credible actionable valuation envelope. A valuation deferral requires a fresh reviewed model, current price, explicit capital/share claims and a broad plausible optimistic envelope (including reasonable narrative/option value). If even that envelope fails the risk-appropriate return hurdle, defer with a price/event reopening condition. This is not proof of a universal upper bound. Unknown bounds must be probed; do not invent a low bull case to suppress research. Material new evidence invalidates this shortcut.

Use revenue-to-owner-profit conversion, unrestricted liquidity versus obligations, dilution, reinvestment/ROIC and paid commercial adoption to prioritize *questions*, conditioned on sector and cycle. Negative FCF, high PE/SBC, falling prices, weak technicals, or absent public unit data alone cannot reject growth/cyclical/financial firms. Technical confluence affects execution confidence, never mechanically multiplies alpha probabilities.

## Validation and budgets
Freeze a small set of economic rules before evaluation. The current17 completed names are development examples, not an independent validation sample. Use chronological forward holdouts across sectors/regimes, including delisted failures and successful turnarounds; never use later filings at an earlier cutoff. Retain a fixed-seed random audit sample of deferred names plus boundary cases. Measure missed material decision changes and risk warnings, not just subsequent price rises. Report uncertainty and false negatives. Reopen after new earnings, new share/debt structures, material claims, a different price zone or changed model regime.

Expected normalized cost = cheap scan cost + selected full/delta cost + independent audits + orchestration/storage overhead. Example: all-name cheap work5% of former full cost plus15% full-equivalent work gives20% total (80% saving). This is planning arithmetic, not evidence that15% selection is sufficient. Never force a pass-rate quota; excess candidates remain explicitly budget-deferred. No fresh buy/size recommendation bypasses applicable full evidence/model/review requirements; reuse still-valid stages and audit only changed dependencies.

Methodological reference: Bailey et al., The Probability of Backtest Overfitting, https://www.davidhbailey.com/dhbpapers/backtest-prob.pdf. Repeated tuning against the same sample invalidates apparently strong results; this paper does not validate our thresholds.
