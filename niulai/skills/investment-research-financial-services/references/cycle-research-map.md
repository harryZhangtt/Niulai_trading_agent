# Cycle research: mechanisms, evidence and tests

Version **2026-10-23-cycle-map-1**. Reviewed 2026-10-23 UTC. This maps the principal decision-relevant families, not every named historical theory. Read with `cycle-capital-allocation.md`, `first-principles-adoption.md` and `cycle-method-integration.md`. Named periods are historical labels, never default forecasts.

## Select mechanisms before methods

| Family | Mechanism and observables | Link to the existing model | Evidence limit / rival explanation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Business cycle | Broad output, employment, income and sales co-movement; breadth, depth and duration | Customer budgets, utilization, credit losses | NBER chronology is retrospective. A macro contraction need not describe a particular industry. [1] |
| Inventory / Kitchin | Delayed orders and stock adjustment; sell-through, inventory/sales, cancellations, producer versus customer stocks | Unit volume, discounts, working capital | Production smoothing and destabilizing adjustment compete; no automatic forty-month recurrence. [2] |
| Investment / Juglar / replacement | Lumpy irreversible capital, replacement vintages, funding and qualification delays | Qualified capacity, ASP, depreciation, maintenance/growth capex | Announced capex is not effective supply. An age distribution or shock can create irregular adjustment rather than a fixed clock. [3] |
| Capital cycle / competitive rents | High returns attract investment; entry barriers, exit, management incentives and substitution alter response | Incremental ROIC, margins, reinvestment, competitive duration | High returns can persist; a dying product need never recover. Use Marathon/Marks/Damodaran as documented in the capital-allocation policy. |
| Credit–property / financial cycle | Credit, collateral values and borrowing capacity reinforce one another; debt service and refinancing later constrain activity | Funding costs, survival, customer demand, dilution | BIS finds equities do not fit its credit/property pattern well; duration and amplitude depend on regime. Do not transplant to equity timing. [4] |
| Minsky financing fragility | Dated contractual cash payments depend increasingly on refinancing or uncertain future receipts | Cash runway, covenant headroom, maturity gaps and tail loss | A balance-sheet stress framework, not a crash-date or probability model. [5] |
| Kuznets / infrastructure / demographics | Household formation, migration and long-lived infrastructure stocks | Local stock/flow demand, replacement, useful life and capital intensity | Historical lineage; country, institutions and one-time urbanization matter. No universal modern long-swing duration. [6] |
| Kondratiev long waves | Candidate interaction of technology, institutions, finance, prices and infrastructure | Conditional long-horizon scenarios, deployment bottlenecks | Contested empirical identification: real/nominal choice and detrending change findings. Few independent historical waves. Do not infer AI inevitability. [7] |
| Schumpeter / Perez technological revolutions | Innovation, complementary infrastructure, financing and institutional adaptation | Time to monetization, complementary capex, distribution of value | Perez's successful historical revolutions are a selected sample, not the denominator of all inventions. Use as a hypothesis generator. [8] |
| Product lifecycle / diffusion / S-curve | Heterogeneous adoption incentives, imitation, saturation, renewal and substitution | Eligible users × paid adoption × retention × net price | Bass is a conditional model, not universal destiny; Klepper documents departures from common industry patterns. Abandonment and niche outcomes remain possible. [9] |
| Productivity J-curve / organizational adaptation | Complementary intangible investment can delay measured productivity | Implementation costs, capitalized versus expensed inputs, time to realized benefit | Initial losses do not prove eventual gains; test complements and measurable benefits against failed deployments. [10] |
| Expectations / risk appetite | Price can anticipate operating changes; funding and sentiment affect required returns | Reverse valuation, market-versus-model expectations, entry opportunity cost | Not a mechanically measurable fixed period. Industry growth and stock returns are different targets. |

For each materially applicable family record: segment/geography, causal link, candidate state, strongest rival, source cutoff, next discriminating observation, exact model line affected and sensitivity. For the remainder record a short reason for inapplicability or uncertainty. Use one card and one integrated model; do not create one agent, score or model per named theory. A permanent decline, structural break, secular expansion and no meaningful cycle are legitimate rival hypotheses. Seasonal variation is separately adjusted and documented.

## Evaluate a paper before borrowing a method

Record primary URL, author/year, access level (full text, author introduction, abstract or metadata only), studied population/period, variable definitions, identification strategy, data revisions, baseline, target loss and limitations. Metadata alone supports attribution, not a detailed empirical claim. Distinguish an explanatory model, a descriptive regularity and a demonstrated forecast improvement. An abstract does not authorize implementing undocumented formulas.

Ask what would refute the result, whether the sample includes failures and discontinued firms, and whether applicability survives changes in industry structure. A high-frequency dataset spanning two long waves does not contain hundreds of independent waves. Document all tried filters, lags and windows; selecting the nicest chart after trying many creates a new statistical problem.

The filter literature has genuine disagreement. Hamilton identifies spurious dynamics and endpoint problems in HP filtering [11]. BIS finds the HP credit gap useful for a specific crisis-warning task despite those objections [12]. Moura shows Hamilton's alternative also introduces dynamics [13]. Therefore neither automatic adoption nor blanket rejection follows: predeclare a target and test on information actually available at each historical origin.

## Lightweight execution and evaluation

1. **Evidence and scope:** recover the existing financial model and source archive; select only decision-relevant mechanisms. Use the six economic questions and first-principles customer/alternative/capture findings. Label old publication vintages and new assumptions.
2. **Point-in-time inputs:** for historical-series claims use `cycle_vintage_checks.py --input PACKET.json --output RESULT.json`. Its expected scope, series definitions, source references, availability and revision identities produce an as-of selected view. Later releases must not alter its selected-view hash. This checks declared metadata, not authenticity or historical archive completeness. Do not silently fabricate release vintages.
3. **Economics:** reuse `cycle_checks.py` for supply-demand, segment cash bridges and normalized/reverse diagnostics; `adoption_checks.py` for task alternatives and missing-outcome accounting. Use existing linked models for financing, equity cash flow and valuation. No phase label can itself change a multiple or alert.
4. **Decision:** reuse trend-to-value joint scenarios and equal-budget buy/stage/wait analysis. Separate customer surplus, vendor capture and per-share return. A changed cycle view must alter a specified assumption, or a numeric comparison must show why the old decision still holds.
5. **Freeze and review:** preserve card/input/output hashes, model bindings, independent critic and actual save/readback. Record the strongest opposing case, symmetric falsifiers and observation deadline. A code pass is not evidence that the thesis is right.
6. **Learn:** reuse `rationale_guard.py score` for mature, frozen, resolvable predictions: Brier loss for binary events, squared error for means, absolute error for medians, interval score for predictive intervals. Compare the same target/units/horizon with a frozen simple baseline. Keep unknown, pending and withdrawn records. Evaluate forecast quality separately from net investment returns and the opportunity cost of waiting. [14]

Use an append-only trial ledger (existing run artifacts/forecast records suffice): `trial_id`, parent/version, hypothesis, issue cutoff, dataset and specification hashes, target/horizon, exploratory or confirmatory status, baseline, evaluation window, outcome version and retired reason. Include abandoned alternatives. Do not build a registry service. Repeatedly inspected holdouts are no longer untouched; adjacent observations or correlated holdings are not independent trials. [15]

Advanced filters, change-point packages and regime models remain optional shadow experiments. Promotion requires a frozen decision-relevant objective, time-ordered or blocked evaluation, uncertainty appropriate to dependence, and useful improvement over a simple baseline after costs. Record every attempted variant. This upgrade implements accounting/provenance safeguards, not empirical proof of better returns, calibrated cycle probabilities or a production cycle predictor.
