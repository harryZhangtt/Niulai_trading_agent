# Equity options: thesis, time, volatility and executable cost

Policy **2026-10-24-equity-options-1**. Use for listed equity option questions and option-versus-share comparisons. Reuse the current underlying company evidence and model, then analyze the derivative separately. This supplements AI Berkshire, the financial-services model, cycle, opportunity-cost and [data-crosscheck policy](data-crosscheck-policy.md); it does not convert the fund-reset model into an options pricer.

## Data admission and access

Prefer an entitled consolidated feed with contract-level quote timestamps and bid/ask sizes. Among reviewed no-extra-data-fee routes, Tradier provides realtime consolidated quotes for eligible brokerage users; its Greeks refresh hourly. Alpaca free indicative quotes are not executable OPRA quotes. Paid OPRA is a comparable consolidated route, not inherently more accurate because its MCP is different. Verify changing entitlement/pricing before use.

Discover and allowlist only quote, chain, expiration, contract-specification and market-calendar methods. Hosted Tradier and Alpaca installations can expose order/account tools; never enable or invoke them merely to research options. Authenticate through supported secure connections, never credentials in chat or source files. Endpoint discovery is not authenticated access. Review/pin any third-party wrapper and retain upstream lineage, raw timestamps and excluded strikes; wrappers stripping these fields are not admitted unmodified. A free MCP is not a market-data license. Do not build an ongoing scraper from a one-off public response.

Record source/feed and entitlement, retrieval and quote times separately, exact contract symbol, underlying, expiration/last-trade convention, strike, right, exercise/settlement style, multiplier/deliverable and adjustment status. Record bid/ask/size, IV/Greek calculation time and convention, OI date and volume. Preserve unknowns. Use two comparable quote routes for critical candidates under the crosscheck policy; a shared OPRA feed is corroboration, not independent market evidence. Disclose missing second-source confirmation.

Research-only historical/indicative inputs can produce conditional arithmetic. They cannot certify an executable limit. Do not substitute last trade for current quotes, response time for quote time, old underlying close for synchronized spot, or assumed midpoint for a fill. Contract-specific adjustments require OCC/broker verification. Standard 100-share assumptions must remain labeled until verified.

## Analyze the trade rather than only the company

1. **Underlying and timing:** recover exact report/model versions and what changed. A present-value equity estimate or 2031/2036 target is not a six-month stock forecast. Build separately justified medium-term catalyst/spot cases, with delay and failure. Verify catalyst dates; estimates are not company confirmations.
2. **Feasible contracts:** compare roughly three/six/nine-month listed expiries when medium term is unspecified, then choose only horizons covering the evidenced thesis. Keep relevant ATM/ITM/OTM alternatives and shares/cash. Do not push the strike far away simply to fit a small budget. A budget is a ceiling, not an obligation to spend it.
3. **Payoff and execution:** run `options_payoff_checks.py` for supported standard long calls and same-expiry bull call debit spreads. Use buy ask/sell bid conservatively, fees, integer contracts, depth when available and unspent cash. Show one contract as well as maximum affordable quantity; affordability is not recommended sizing. Compare equal-budget shares, with fractional ownership only if supported. Exact expiry payoff is distinct from pre-expiry resale value and physical exercise funding.
4. **Volatility and time:** run the small `options_scenario_checks.py` European BSM diagnostic for supported sensitivities only. State continuous yield, rate, ACT/365 time and per-share/contract scaling. Use separate strike IVs and a spot × elapsed time × IV grid including crush, widening and slow realization. Vega is dollars per one volatility percentage point, theta per calendar day. Reprice large moves; delta-only extrapolation is insufficient. This helper is not an American pricer, calibrated surface or executable fair-value estimate.
5. **Model adequacy:** US equity options normally allow early exercise. For a definitive recommendation where early exercise, discrete dividends, borrow/carry or material numerical differences matter, use a pinned validated American engine (e.g. QuantLib) with convergence and independently checked benchmarks. Do not silently approximate discrete dividends with continuous yield. A zero-dividend American call comparison still requires verified dividend/carry assumptions and the theorem's conditions. The optional pinned QuantLib 1.44 adapter is now installed and tested; follow [model selection](options-model-selection.md). Its presence does not validate dividends, quotes or the trade thesis.
6. **Value versus price:** distinguish risk-neutral IV/delta/densities from physical profit probabilities. Use supported spot/time scenarios or explicit sensitivities and switching probabilities; do not claim the highest expected return from unknown probabilities. Recompute maximum acceptable debit from that thesis and costs. High absolute IV alone does not prove an option overpriced; compare likely event/realized variation and premium at the relevant horizon.
7. **Operations:** a long call costing less than $1,000 can require many thousands to exercise. A bounded expiration spread diagram does not remove early assignment, pin risk or temporary stock exposure. Assess broker permissions and an explicit pre-expiry package close if funding is insufficient; do not promise fills or place orders. Include planned exit, maximum premium-at-risk and invalidation.
8. **Independent review and persistence:** retain rejected candidates, raw input hashes, exact calculators/revisions, comparison results, scenario assumptions, quote gaps and independent arithmetic/source review. Finalize only the scope actually satisfied. Missing live data or horizon evidence leaves an actionable recommendation incomplete even if diagnostic arithmetic is complete. Existing full underlying gates remain applicable; derivative diagnostics do not newly certify an old baseline as full research.

## Executable completion gate

Set `instrument_profile="equity_options"` and the versioned `options_evidence` block before finalization; `subject.security_type="equity_options"` also triggers the validator and cannot silently omit the explicit profile. `options_evidence.py` is called by both `finalize_research.py` and `research_completion.py`. The adjacent `test_options_evidence.py` fixture documents the current interface with synthetic inputs; it is not live research evidence.

Actionable records require the full underlying-company profile, exact contract source bindings, current method hashes, reproducible payoff and per-leg scenario pairs, adverse price/time/IV/combined cases, source-backed quote-side crosschecks no weaker than the quote freshness policy, and a canonical payoff graphic embedded in the report. An independent review must bind the actual artifacts and explain contract terms, source lineage, IV, exercise/carry, model adequacy, horizon thesis, catalysts/falsifiers, decision rationale and rendered presentation. Existing persistence/readback and full underlying gates still apply. Successful structural validation does not certify source truth, fills, accurate subjective probabilities or a platform-wide inability to bypass undeclared workflows.

For historical/indicative or externally blocked comparisons, use `status="partial"`, `options_evidence.status="research_only"`, explicit blockers and `result.recommendation="none"`. The validator rejects claiming this is complete/actionable. The report can still give useful conditional arithmetic and explain why a purchase is not supported. Do not fill missing data with invented timestamps, deliverables or probabilities just to clear the gate.

## Required beginner-readable decision display

Show the underlying separately from each canonical option symbol, BUY/SELL, contracts, expiry, strike, call/put, exercise/settlement and multiplier verification. Decode one symbol in plain language. A spread is a package, not an instruction to sell an uncovered call. Label all premium figures per share versus per contract and net package debit; show assumed broker fees separately. State whether the figures are historical displayed prices, executable quotes or model values.

Prominently show (1) exact expiration P&L with zero line, break-even, max-loss and cap versus same-budget shares/cash; (2) spot × time × IV scenarios before expiry; (3) IV skew/term nodes or a sparse heatmap with missing observations left blank. An interpolated surface is optional, never a substitute for observed data. Include a plain-language takeaway for each figure and do not rely on color alone. Green/red mean gain/loss, not buy/sell recommendations. A chart generated from checked numeric inputs must retain its input hashes and independent review; a decorative chart does not satisfy the completion guard.

## Volatility surfaces: add only when justified

Begin with strike-specific bid/ask IV intervals, term structure, obvious quote inconsistencies and event scenarios. Do not fit five or more surface parameters to a sparse asynchronous chain. Gatheral–Jacquier SVI provides useful calendar/butterfly discipline for European/forward-normalized prices; raw American equity quotes need exercise/dividend treatment first. A curve passing arbitrage tests still need not forecast future IV or earn excess returns. Preserve failed fits, parameter searches and out-of-time tests. Never interpret a fitted smile as independent confirmation of the underlying thesis.

Existing professional choices reviewed: QuantLib American lattice/finite-difference engines (modified BSD); vollib BSM/Black IV/Greeks (MIT, European toolkit). QuantLib 1.44 is installed behind the optional tested American adapter; vollib remains a reviewed candidate, not an installed dependency. Do not install both or Heston/local-vol/Monte Carlo merely to compare one small directional trade. Core payoff/BSM/surface checks remain standard-library based. Follow [model selection](options-model-selection.md) for actual interfaces and limits.
