# Entry policy and opportunity cost

Version **2026-10-23-entry-policy-2**. Apply to new or materially revised buy/wait/entry decisions. Preserve prior completed records without retroactive certification. A 12% hurdle is a stated preference or sensitivity, not an empirically optimal universal threshold.

## Required comparison

Compare **buy now, a small starter plus conditional remainder, and wait** using one initial budget, one valuation date, one calendar end date, the same fundamental/market scenarios and consistent taxes/costs. Include cash carry or an explicitly specified alternative investment; do not silently assume idle cash earns zero or that an alternative has risk-free equity returns. State distributions, reinvestment, fills, exposure and terminal exit method. Compare expected terminal wealth and paired regret, downside/loss and liquidity constraints; do not choose by mean alone. Keep portfolio overlap and marginal risk separate from security-level EV. Allocation fractions are assumptions until justified by loss budget and concentration.

Treat never-filled rising paths, early fills followed by deterioration, late fills, chop, and favorable rapid growth symmetrically. A lower price conditional on damaged fundamentals is not the old upside at a cheaper price. Apply predeclared thesis invalidation and review rules to every policy, including positions already bought. Price alerts remain review triggers, not automatic trades.

Use **the same information set**: freeze policy parameters before scenarios/outcomes, allow decisions to depend only on observations already available, retain point-in-time evidence and revisions. Never infer threshold-hit probabilities, ordering or fill quality from terminal DCF values or daily OHLC. Avoid hindsight entry at the exact low, survivor selection, multiple parameter searches and tuning scenario weights to the user's desired decision. Distinguish judgmental stress paths from a calibrated stochastic model.

## Computation and evidence

Run `references/niulai-workflow/scripts/entry_policy_ev.py --input INPUT --output OUTPUT` for explicit discrete observation scenarios, or an independently audited equivalent for dividends, taxes, real execution or fund mechanics not supported by the helper. The helper is a **scenario calculator**, not a market backtester: prices must be split-adjusted price-only observations, dividends separate, not total-return/adjusted-close prices. Its threshold orders execute at the first eligible supplied observation price; no intraperiod crossing or historical live-fill claim is supported. Future paths and rates are inputs, not estimated by the tool.

Supply fixed policies `{id, kind: now|staged|wait, starter_fraction, limit_price}` with all three kinds, nonnegative cash rate assumption or a rate greater than -100%, fees, initial budget, horizon and `invalidation_action: liquidate|hold_no_new`. Each scenario supplies strictly chronological `{time_years,price,dividend_per_share,invalidated}` observations, including time zero and the common horizon. Dividends at an observation belong only to pre-existing holdings. On invalidation, no new purchase is allowed; optional liquidation applies consistently. Document invalidation evidence availability and an executable observation price, not a retrospective label. Use `probability_basis: unknown|judgment|calibrated`; only the latter may claim calibration, and requires external `calibration_evidence`. Unknown probabilities must omit weights: return payoff bounds, scenario dominance and switching conditions, not invented EV. Known weights must cover all scenarios and sum to one. For two scenarios, report paired break-even probability; label it the probability at which a policy ranking changes, not an estimated probability. Bound scenario coverage; worst observed loss is not the worst possible loss.

## Company-bound completion gate

For each new/material entry reassessment, first recover and hash actual prior model inputs/outputs and supporting sources. Reuse valid evidence; fill decision-relevant gaps before concluding. A synthetic example or “no evidence to change the price” is insufficient to certify unchanged tiers. Both unchanged and changed decisions require a calculated comparison and a clear numerical rationale. There is no quota for price changes or positive alpha.

Pin `policy_versions.entry_policy: 2026-10-23-entry-policy-2`. The `entry_policy_evidence` block requires `version`, `status: compared`, `basis: company_bound`, `probability_basis: judgment|calibrated`, `common_horizon_years`, `initial_budget`, `information_cutoff`, `limitations`, `baseline_artifact_ids`, `source_evidence_artifact_ids`, `input_artifact_id`, `output_artifact_id`, `review_artifact_id`, and `decision: {change: changed|unchanged, selected_policy: now|staged|wait, rationale}`. These artifact IDs resolve through the record's hashed artifacts. Preserve recovered baseline versions in their provenance.

The input and output JSON both carry `ticker`, `basis: company_bound`, the same `probability_basis`, `common_horizon_years` and `initial_budget`. The input's `baseline_artifact_hashes` exactly pins baseline + source IDs. The output pins `input_sha256` and has `policies.{now,staged,wait}.{expected_terminal_wealth,loss_probability,nonfill_probability}`, a nonempty `sensitivity` list and the same `decision` object. An adapter may bind an existing independently audited calculation into this representation; it must not fabricate results. The review JSON carries ticker, independent `reviewer_id`, `status: approved_company_entry`, baseline/source/input/output `artifact_hashes` and positive `numerical_check_count`. Calibrated claims additionally require `calibration_artifact_ids`; artifact existence does not establish genuine calibration.

Run `references/niulai-workflow/scripts/entry_policy_evidence.py --record RECORD`. The gate is also invoked by `finalize_research.py` and `research_completion.py`; schema structure is checked separately. It validates file hashes, cross-artifact bindings, finite comparison metrics and independent review metadata, not economic truth or future forecast accuracy. New entry runs must declare the version; this preserves old records without retroactive certification. Price-only output is exempt.

Unknown path probabilities do not justify inventing certainty: specify and stress a transparent judgmental model, evaluate direction under alternative defensible assumptions, and distinguish that from empirical calibration. If the actual comparison cannot be completed, use `status: limited|pending` in a non-complete record and explicitly identify remaining work. A limited synthetic-only calculation cannot certify the new recommendation or an unchanged tier. Complete recoverable evidence/model work in the active request instead of ending with that label.

## Learning a rule without overfitting

Do not promote two stocks or a synthetic example into a general rule. Pre-register candidate policies, eligibility and metrics; use a broad point-in-time universe including failures/delistings, multiple cycles and sector strata, realistic spreads, corporate actions, data availability and nonfills. Separate training, validation and untouched time-forward test periods; purge overlapping holding-period leakage, cluster uncertainty by time/sector, report effective independent sample size, and compare to simple cash/buy-now baselines. Penalize parameter search, show sensitivity rather than one winning setting, and validate prospectively before promotion. Track forecast error, probability calibration, regret and drawdown separately. Until such evidence exists, enforce the **comparison process**, not a claimed winning strategy. Preserve null results and reasoned no-edge conclusions.

Persist methodology revisions with tests and independent review. Never change active alerts, probabilities, hurdle preferences or scheduled research merely to make a comparison look better; alert changes require the existing authorized CRUD gate.
