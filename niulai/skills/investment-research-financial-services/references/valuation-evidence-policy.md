# Earnings, probabilities and entry confirmation

Policy: 2026-10-20-valuation-evidence-1. Apply to every new or resumed full recommendation. Reuse the installed growth-aware AI Berkshire and Anthropic modeling workflows; do not modify pinned upstream copies. Previously published records retain their historical policy, not retroactive certification.

## Earnings and business values
Build revenue from economically meaningful drivers by material business: units/installed base/utilization, net price, mix, renewals and acquisitions. Bridge segment revenue through variable costs, fixed costs, taxes, recurring SBC once, working capital, capex and financing to owner cash per share. Separate reported figures, management targets and analyst assumptions. Reconcile segments, shared costs, eliminations, net debt and diluted claims to the consolidated result. Show each business's attributable value and valuation method where identifiable. Where segments share assets or disclosure is insufficient, show bounded allocations and sensitivity, or explain why SOTP is not identifiable; never fabricate segment margins or double-count installed-base services and equipment. For funds use material underlying exposure and wrapper economics, not corporate SOTP of the wrapper.

Check past forecasts against actuals when dated forecasts exist, with forecast horizon, bias/error and structural-break limitations. No available forecast history means unvalidated forecast accuracy, not accurate by construction. More granular forecasts do not inherently improve accuracy.

## Probabilities and professional research
Read accessible original professional/fund methodology and relevant company/industry research; record document, date, passages, actual application and disagreements. Paywall/snippet-only access is a gap, not a report read. Use historical base rates with explicit reference class, sample dates, sample size and regime applicability. Separate priors, new evidence and posterior judgment. Do not invent Bayes likelihoods or call subjective weights calibrated. Sum scenario probabilities to one; preserve plausible strong growth and adverse paths. Recompute EV and entry ranges under alternative weights and correlated drivers; report when a decision flips. When probabilities are unidentifiable, provide robustness bounds rather than false precision. Treat required return as an investment choice distinct from risk-appropriate fair value, and avoid double-counting risk in cashflows and discount rates. Include price-implied expectations and the strongest supported case for the market price.

## Technical confirmation, separate from value
Compute fundamental entry zones independently before comparing split-adjusted price/volume history. Record provider, data cutoff, adjustment method, lookback, reproducible rule and parameter sensitivity. Inspect support/resistance, prior platforms/major swings, volume and relative strength only where supported by actual data. No lookahead or selecting thresholds to fit the desired entry. Daily volume profiles are approximations, not true intraday traded-at-price observations.

For each entry tier show fundamental zone, nearby technical zone (or none), overlap, confirmation, invalidation, proposed incremental and total position, and waiting/opportunity cost. Fundamental value plus technical support is not two independent proofs of alpha. Correlated indicators do not multiply confidence; do not raise fundamental scenario probabilities without validated predictive evidence. No overlap is a valid result. Broken support can justify smaller left-side entry or waiting, not a universal prohibition. Missing history must be marked unavailable and prevents claims of confirmed technical support; it need not prevent a clearly conditional fundamental wait decision. Leveraged fund levels require underlying signals, reset/path/cost and corporate-action checks; never treat a long-lived wrapper price line as stable intrinsic value.

## Required machine-readable evidence
Every full record must include `valuation_evidence` with this `version` and five sections: `business_values`, `forecast_validation`, `probability_basis`, `professional_research`, `technical_confirmation`. Each section has `status` (`evidenced`, `limited`, `not_applicable`), substantive `finding`, nonempty `artifact_refs` resolving to the record's hashed artifacts, and nonempty `limitations` when not evidenced. Independent review must inspect these artifacts and verify calculations and provenance; boolean acknowledgements alone are insufficient. A limited section may support a conditional wait/no_buy decision, not an unsupported certainty claim. Finalization and registration reject missing sections for new publications. Archived publications before policy activation retain their actual historical standard.
