---
name: information-catalog
description: Build and expand a source-grounded information-question plan for company or industry research using the supplied evidence inventory and retrieval capabilities. Does not approve conclusions or authorize new tools.
---

The application supplies the question, cutoff, existing catalog context, capability
registry, output schema and budget. Return the information plan in that schema.
Questions are research needs, not findings. A registered provider is not proof of
access, successful retrieval or relevant coverage.

Start from the business question and available old context. Include the required
facets without copying a universal per-ticker source checklist. Distinguish prior
notes, original documents and unknown history. If a mechanism is already covered,
ask what changed or which missing evidence would discriminate between explanations.

Use source roles before named providers: original specification or release notes,
contracts/pricing terms, filings, customer operations, independent measurements,
competitors or research papers when relevant. Select actual capability IDs from
the supplied registry. Record an access/capability need when the useful source is
unsupported; proposing a URL does not enable its host or configure a connector.

Develop breadth across mechanisms, commercialization, adoption and adverse evidence.
Develop depth around a specific unresolved link. A claim of interoperability can
lead to protocol/version compatibility, identity and permissions, actual invocation,
latency, concurrency, state recovery and operational limits. Choose the links
material to this question; do not demand a paper or repository for every company.
An employee demonstration is distinct from inspected proprietary runtime code or
an independently replicated production test.

For use-case economics ask for the baseline, measured population, period, units,
implementation and ongoing costs, sponsor and attribution limits. For adoption,
separate announcements, pilots, production use, paid purchases and renewals. Keep
asset/usage counts, ACV/ARR, forecasts and recognized revenue distinct. Seek the
specific absence or adverse evidence that could contradict the mechanism.

After reading, expand only when an observed source exposes an important new entity,
dependency or contradiction. Give the expansion reason and retain all parent
questions; record unfilled needs instead of deleting them. Stop at the supplied
budget or when the stated evidence need is met. A failed request is not evidence
that nothing changed. A repeated or syndicated disclosure adds no independent
confirmation.

Historical tasks use only the supplied time-gated packet. Do not use later product
names, current search snippets or model memory as factual evidence. Unknown dates,
mutable current pages and old titles attached to newer bodies require separate
version evidence. Do not backdate retrieved data or infer historical novelty from
what the current agent has not seen.

The plan compiler validates identity, bounds, dependencies and retained questions.
It does not certify that this plan is complete or that its future sources support
a claim. Fetching, semantic review and accepted snapshots remain separate steps.
