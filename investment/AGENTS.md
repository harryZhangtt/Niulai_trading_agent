# Investment evidence execution

For new full company research, separate the frozen evidence requirements from
source acquisition. Read `research/README.md`. Use `investment.research` to freeze
the claim/source requirements before fetching. The coordinator retains the returned
expected hash outside the author packet. Fetchers and discovery results cannot
edit this snapshot; a changed scope needs a new snapshot and explicit review.

Use `shared.research_sources` for typed source acquisition, plus the existing web
search workflow for broad orientation and contrary evidence. Select only the
capabilities that help the actual question; do not fan out every tool by default.
OpenAlex discovery and search snippets do not count as reading a document.

Full NVDA company research uses `accelerated-compute`; its source plan includes
issuer technical documents, relevant methods/results papers, comparative technical
evidence and business realization. The initial issuer host override is NVDA only.
Do not silently apply NVIDIA hosts to another issuer. Other industries use a
reviewed applicable profile; `business-economics` is a small nontechnical pilot,
not a universal substitute for material technical questions. Keep the complete
material question roster and the original required full-research subjects.

Publish/complete through `investment.research.pipeline.finalize` and `complete`
(or their CLI operations). Both require the frozen coordinator contract and verify
source coverage alongside the preserved Niulai validators. Do not invoke the old
migration scripts directly to bypass the additional gate. Preserve the snapshot
and archive. These are supported execution entry points, not an OS-level sandbox
or a control over other hosts/cloud tasks.

Actual passages, source revision/availability, source lineage, economic/model
bindings and an independently performed, hash-bound review are required. A reviewer
must inspect support, methods/workloads, conflicting evidence and economic transfer.
Do not fabricate approval fields to pass the validator. Source counts, fetched
bytes, hashes and review flags do not independently prove truth or investment alpha.
