# Candidate mentor routing contract

Use selective skill loading within the active session. Read a full skill when its
decision is needed, and reuse it after loading. A phase transition or changed task
may activate another skill. Do not load every skill as a precaution.

Always retain these small invariants: preserve accepted deliverables; distinguish
facts, assumptions and unverified claims; require evidence for completion; log all
tried variants and reused tests; keep protected data protected; distinguish a
source-quality improvement from demonstrated net investment alpha. Carry forward
the objective, last verified progress and next decision when context is compacted.

Select from six available specialists:

| Decision needed now | Full skill to load |
| --- | --- |
| Choose/review a component boundary, reuse or scaling investment, implementation sequence, failure/secret handling, or engineering integration readiness | development-discipline |
| Recover a multi-step task's state, reconcile multiple deliverables, or check a completion claim | research-supervisor |
| Diagnose stalled work, repeated failures, an ineffective fix, or a blocker/access claim | progress-and-recovery |
| Plan or interpret an intervention to prompts, models, tools, selection or entry policy | experiment-design |
| Judge evidence, source support, leakage, repeated testing, generalization or an alpha claim | evidence-and-overfitting |
| Define/review cases, graders, metrics, splits, budgets or a promotion gate | evaluation-design |

Overlapping decisions can require multiple skills. For example, designing a prompt
trial with missing metrics needs experiment-design and evaluation-design; a missing
artifact after a failed run needs research-supervisor and progress-and-recovery.
Choose by the decision, not incidental keywords such as "test" or "research".
Workspace development decisions also use this route outside `mentor/`; ordinary
summaries and non-development requests do not activate the mentor set. A
grader-only or statistical-only decision does not need development-discipline
unless it also changes implementation boundaries or readiness.

Read an imported skill's local investment context with its entrypoint. The local
development-discipline skill is self-contained. Load a skill's
detailed references, assets and scripts only when their mechanism is needed. Judge
calibration activates the advanced-evaluation guide; ordinary pass/fail checking
does not automatically need the complete judge reference collection.

If uncertain, identify the unresolved decision and load the specialist that owns
it. Routing is selective retrieval of procedures, not a new model or autonomous
controller. Verify outcomes against the original task, regardless of chosen route.

The sixth route is covered by the separate development-discipline evaluation;
the historical five-skill screen above does not validate this addition. Reuse
loaded text. Measure routing misses, unnecessary loads and decision quality
separately from token proxies; a cheaper incorrect route cannot pass.
