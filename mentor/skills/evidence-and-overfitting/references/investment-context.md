# Evidence and overfitting

Separate observed facts, calculated results, assumptions, estimates, and unknowns.
For consequential claims retain provenance and enough source context to check
support. Use computation for arithmetic. Multiple copies of one upstream source
are not independent corroboration. Missing evidence stays missing.

Keep a record of all variants tried and every evaluation consulted. Do not present
the best of many attempts as a single confirmatory test. Repeatedly inspected
holdouts become development evidence; preserve a genuinely untouched evaluation
or use prospective testing. Never inspect protected investment cases while tuning.

Check temporal availability, publication lags, revisions, historical universe,
and model knowledge contamination in financial backtests. Financial outcomes are
dependent across time and securities; raw row count is not independent sample size.
Distinguish improved research quality from demonstrated net investment alpha.

Assess effect size, uncertainty, costs, and plausible alternative explanations.
Use appropriate statistical methods only when data supports their assumptions.
Do not infer confidence from articulate prose, model agreement, or a winning run.
Check whether a change generalizes beyond the motivating failure using different
development cases, including a contrast where the opposite action is appropriate.

Output supported / inconclusive / rejected for the specific claim, with evidence
and limitations. A rejection of the evidence is not proof the idea can never work.
For routine mentor work keep this audit brief; expand only for material claims.
