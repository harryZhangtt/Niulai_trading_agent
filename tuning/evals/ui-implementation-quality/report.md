## What was actually verified

The fresh Dispatch Desk challenge was authored separately from the previous
eight-case profile fixture. Real Chromium execution checked:

- 320px layout with long unbroken notice/history content, without hiding it;
- late decoded responses and failures across entity selection;
- immutable write payloads, newer drafts and other-entity drafts during saves;
- synchronous double activation and unknown outcomes after a committed write;
- malformed service data, useful rejection feedback and synthetic diagnostic
  suppression in page text and browser logs;
- an exported preview that updates from props with no service reads/writes;
- actual supplied expensive-history executions during twelve draft keystrokes.

Both repairs fixed the uncontested cases, including every declared critical
state/write/diagnostic case. The seed performed twelve extra expensive history
executions during typing; both repaired implementations satisfied the bound of
at most one. Both previews render supplied props/history independently of the
service. The general implementation extracts a controller hook and domain
parsers; the routed implementation keeps its controller in the app module with
separate data validation and render-only preview. File count is not a score.
These observations do not prove complete architectural separation or universal
render efficiency beyond the exercised boundary and workload.

Initial inspections in both arms identified actual source mechanisms across
layout, races/writes/drafts, payload/error boundaries, preview coupling and
expensive rendering. Their issue lists group mechanisms differently, so six
versus eight report entries must not be called a difference in defects found.
Worker-authored tests and screenshots are retained separately; their self-report
counts were not substituted for independent frozen browser outcomes.

## Grader defect discovered before interpreting the result

Frozen Q04 correctly detects stale Harbor alerts, but also requires pristine
Ridge Save to be enabled. The public task does not require an unchanged notice
to create another permanent revision. The general repair intentionally disables
Save until a valid edit exists, including in a healthy session. That policy
caused its original failure; there was no observed late-error poisoning.

A blinded author audit received anonymous unchanged app copies, not arm labels,
worker packets, skill exposure or original worker scores. The separate
`q04-validity-diagnostic-v1` ran twelve scenarios: healthy selection, late failure
on a pristine selection, and editing before the late failure, across seed,
reference and both repairs. Every valid edited save committed exactly once to
Ridge with exact content and unchanged Harbor state. Both repairs had no stale
alert; the seed retained its real stale-alert defect. All 54 before/after source
fingerprints matched. Ports were closed after execution.

Original tests, gold and scores were not rewritten. Q04 is flagged as an invalid
policy-dependent comparison item. The other eleven checks are 11/11 for both
repairs. The diagnostic is post-result measurement repair, not a new blind
efficacy experiment or a replacement 12/12 baseline score. A new grader version
requires a new identity and a fresh preregistered comparison. Do not use this
historical runner as an unqualified universal promotion gate.

## Repeatable evidence and limits

`prepare.py` checks the author's frozen hashes and refuses existing worker
packets. `grade.py` runs the immutable TypeScript/build/browser checks on a
finished patch and preserves every result. `summarize.py` validates source,
control, patch and diagnostic fingerprints; retains raw scores; and reports the
measurement defect, routing, quality and context separately. Five deterministic
grader/review tests reject incomplete/duplicate/retried/skipped cases, global
startup failure and immutable-file changes, and prevent the known false failure
from creating a quality-advantage claim. The review regression is separate from
the original frozen grader tests. Aggregation exit 0 means artifact validation
completed; its explicit effectiveness verdict remains **inconclusive**.

Source is under `tuning/evals/ui-implementation-quality/`; generated packets,
repairs, controls, screenshots, browser receipts and the blinded diagnostic are
under ignored `tuning/runtime/ui-implementation-quality-v1/`. `results.json`
contains case-level evidence. This is instruction-level file isolation, not an
OS-enforced holdout. Cases are synthetic author-created development material and
are now exposed. Model prior knowledge and host skill metadata were not erased.

This trial supports the routed implementation's scoped behavior and correct
selection, while leaving additional skill effectiveness inconclusive. It does
not establish production backend idempotency, cross-tab/restart recovery, full
accessibility, general visual quality or safety of arbitrary future apps. No
production runtime, provider, credentials or business email was exercised.
The separate UI SessionStart definition has its own unverified host trust and
scheduled/email acceptance state; these browser results do not accept it.
